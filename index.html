Privacy Policy for Calcular Bundle

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains what information the Calcular Bundle Android app (package com.dhyanjyoti.calculators) handles, why, and the choices you have. Calcular Bundle is developed by Dhyanjyoti Das (“we”, “us”).

1. Summary

  • You don’t need an account, and the app never asks for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your calculations are done on your phone and stay on your phone. We do not run a server and never receive them.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google’s ad service collects some device information, described in section 3.

2. Information stored on your device

To remember your work, the app saves the values you enter, your calculation history and your recently used calculators only on your device. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and sends to Google:

  • your device’s IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • information about your interactions with ads, such as ads viewed and tapped;
  • diagnostic and device information, such as app performance, device model and operating system.

This information is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and it is sent over encrypted connections. Depending on your choices and where you live, it may be used to show personalised ads. The app requests family-friendly ad content only.

Google handles this information under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

The app does not include any other analytics or tracking tools. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

4. Your choices

  • Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google’s consent form when the app first opens. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy options.
  • Everyone can reset or delete their advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in Android settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). The app keeps working normally either way.

5. Data retention and deletion

We do not collect or keep any of your data. Information stored on your device stays there until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app. Information collected by Google for advertising is kept and deleted according to Google’s privacy policy linked above.

6. Children

Calcular Bundle is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us.

7. Security

Your calculations never leave your device. Advertising data is sent to Google over encrypted (TLS) connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable care to protect your information.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the “Last updated” date above.
